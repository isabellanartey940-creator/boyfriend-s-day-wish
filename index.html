A Letter From My Heart

Written for you, my love.

To the Love of My Life,

My dearest,

Thou art truly the sweetest blessing that hath ever found its way into my life. Words, no matter how many I may write, could never fully express the depth of love I carry for thee. ♡

I know that at times I may fail to show it, yet believe me when I say—I love thee dearly. More than I sometimes know how to say, and more than my actions may reveal.

I shall never forget how thou didst so quietly and unexpectedly slip into my life. Yet, I believe with all my heart that God knew exactly what He was doing when He brought thee to me.

I may not always be beside thee when thou needest me most, and there may have been quarrels between us, tears, and even moments when I threatened to walk away. But through it all, remember this: I have thee.

No matter the number of storms we weather, thou shalt always hold a most precious place within my heart.

Thou art kind.
Thou art amazing.
Thou art handsome.
Thou art smart.
Thou art worthy.

My sweet boy, thou deservest every beautiful thing this life hath to offer, and so much more. ♡

And if these words should ever fade with the passing of years, I pray the love behind them never shall.

I love thee.
I love thee.
I love thee—more than these little words could ever tell.

Yours, truly,
Your Childhood Cinderella ♡
